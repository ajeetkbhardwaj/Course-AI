### 🗣️ **Learn and Build (Speech Recognition) Systems**

* **Speech recognition (ASR / STT)** : converting  **spoken speech into text** .
* One of the most widely used and exciting **spoken language processing** tasks.
* **Common applications:**
  * **Dictation** (voice-to-text typing).
  * **Voice assistants** (Siri, Google Assistant, Alexa).
  * **Video captioning** .
  * **Meeting transcription** .
* **How voice assistants work:**
  * First step :  **transcribe speech to text** .
  * Then, the transcribed text is used for downstream tasks (e.g., answering questions, finding weather info).
* **Challenges in speech recognition:**
  * **Background noise** in audio recordings.
  * **Accents and pronunciation differences** between speakers.
  * **Punctuation and symbols** don’t have clear acoustic cues.
  * Requires  **joint understanding of audio and language** .
* **Goal of this section:**
  * Learn how **Transformers** can be applied to **speech-to-text** conversion.
  * Understand and use **pre-trained speech recognition models** from the 🤗  **Transformers library** .
  * Learn how to **fine-tune ASR models** for specific  **domains or languages** .
  * Build a **live demo** that converts spoken speech into text.
* **Objectives:**
  * Identify suitable  **pre-trained ASR models** .
  * **Choose and prepare datasets** for speech recognition.
  * Understand **evaluation metrics** for ASR performance.
  * **Fine-tune** an ASR model using the  **Trainer API** .
  * **Build and deploy** a demo that performs real-time  **speech-to-text transcription** .
  * Complete a **hands-on exercise** to apply these concepts.
